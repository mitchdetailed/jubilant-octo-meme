## 5. Deviations from the design

1. **The undo stack in `esses_qtmodel` needs Qt Gui** (D-161, `DECISIONS.md` A.13, proposed). In Qt 6
   `QUndoStack`/`QUndoCommand` are Qt Gui classes; `esses_qtmodel` now links Qt Gui and may include only those two
   headers from it (`tools/check_layering.py` rule 6; `cmake/qt-free-check.cmake` still refuses Qt Widgets, OpenGL,
   Svg and PrintSupport there). Closes M1 open issue 9.

2. **`project-1` minor 2** (D-162, `DECISIONS.md` A.13, proposed): the Data-window filter keys are `"default"` or
   the hash text `xxh3:<32 hex>`; the step `project-1` 1 -> 2 is the first real migration of 07 §5.1 (bare hex keys
   gain the prefix, any other key moves to `x-esses-legacy.dataWindowFilters`). 02 §6.2's key table is updated.
3. **Load pipeline of the project documents** (D-163, proposed): the typed reader runs before `SchemaRegistry`,
   which validates the written form (02 §6.1 writes "validate -> migrate -> parse"); a newer minor keeps its
   `schemaVersion` on save and is refused as `Unsupported` when this build cannot read all of it (closes R-38).

4. **Maths dimension warnings** (D-164, proposed): a bare numeric literal takes the dimension of what it meets
   (no `W-DIM-MISMATCH` for `'Speed'[km/h] > 100`), and an item that leaves its unit and quantity at the
   unitless defaults declares no result unit (no `W-RESULT-DIM`); refines 03 §3.2 rules 4 and 10.
5. **Maths diagnostics beyond 03 §2.5 and the nesting limit** (D-165, proposed): `E-SYNTAX`, `E-EXPR-EMPTY`,
   `E-UNEXPECTED-CHAR`, `E-COMMENT-UNCLOSED`, `E-NUMBER-FORMAT`, `E-NAME-EMPTY`, `E-EXPR-TOO-DEEP` (256 levels)
   and `E-KERNEL-UNAVAILABLE`.
6. **`esses-cli eval` on the calling thread** (D-166, proposed), over logged channels and details; maths outputs
   come with `MathGraph::define`.
7. **Where `compile`, `evaluateWhole` and `MathInputGatherer::fill` live**: in `libs/maths/src/lang/` with the
   `EwKernel` they share, not in `src/kernels/` as §2's contract table lists them (the headers stay put); the
   stage-input convention `makeIntrinsicKernel` must follow is in `libs/maths/README.md` ("The language").

8. **Text engine as built** (D-167, proposed): the `@auto` names row at half numeric, decimal comma among separator tokens, rate candidates checked against the time column, column classification from spread samples, a portable multi-line sniff search, the `capture` direction by `keyValueColumns`, the slab decoder's in-flight table as the share key (siblings through `store::WeakColumn`), the `blocks` layout Unsupported until the Pi profile (`docs/formats/text.md`); its "no page-fault guard for text files" is withdrawn by D-190.
9. **Step 4 and the Mapping-panel hand-off as built** (D-168, proposed): column hints and the dictionary (4a), the unit map before the registry (4b), load-time mappings (4c); unresolved = an unknown unit or a quantity without a unit, so unit-less CSVs open at once; timestamped channels in the pipeline; `withBuiltins` registers the shipped text profiles; fixture-1 oracle kind `generator`; the Qt-free `bench/formats_bench` measures the formats share of first paint.

10. **`MathGraph` as built** (D-171, proposed): built-in outputs first and transient maths last in a shadowing chain,
   disabled elements skipped by consumers and by the next element, module conditions folded over the details and the
   module's constants (`E-CONDITION` when they do not fold), alias fall-through on definition errors only, a maths
   output's descriptor unit as stored (the base unit of the declared unit), a `builtin` item overriding the
   registered module's settings for its source, and the graph surface Channel Status needs (`nodes()`,
   `status(NodeId)`, `invalidate`, `setResolverTables`, `setCompat`, `setDistanceIndexes`).

11. **IIR filters designed in-tree** (D-169, proposed): no Iir1; RC sections and Chebyshev type I/Butterworth second-order sections with the readings `iir.*` of `docs/maths-compat.md` (ripple between 1 and 1 + r/100, `fc` at the ripple-band edge, unit gain at DC/Nyquist/centre, band filters of twice the prototype order, steady-state priming, re-priming after gaps over 1 s).
12. **Kernels as built** (D-170, proposed): `Kernel::status()` reports failures known only at evaluation (`E-ARG-VALUE` at or above half the result rate, `E-RANGE-UNKNOWN` naming the source's paths; `RangeTables::contains`/`paths`); `compile()` gives `integrate_dist` the scope's `Corr Dist` as a plan input (`E-NO-DISTANCE` without it); the case corpus lives in `tests/maths/cases/` with schema `maths-case-1` and is generated by `tools/maths_ref.py` (not `tests/maths/reference/`); `mathfn-manifest-1` is written.

13. **Undo, persistence and the journal in `esses_qtmodel`** (D-172, proposed): whole-document commands; an undone options
   edit keeps the view state (`lastSelection`, filters, MRU, the stretch toggle); the F9 domain stays view state (D-032
   over 02 §7); the workbook list is a document (`WorkbookSet`); trust edits from the Data window and the Mapping panel's
   decision are undoable; dirty for the journal = canonical text differs from the file; save writes changed documents
   only, retires files the project no longer names, never writes over an unreadable document; autosave is suspended
   while a recovery decision is outstanding.
14. **`MathsService` and Channel Status as built** (D-173, proposed): `esses.gps_corrections` registered where both GPS
   inputs are logged; local maths from the sidecar on every snapshot; the live check's scope; Channel Status rows and
   kinds beyond 06 §10; `channelReady` once per output and version.
15. **Lap stretching in 0.1** (D-174, proposed): the track length is the source's `Venue Length` (or an explicit value)
   until track maps; `LapStretchService` publishes `Range::stretch`; a stretch-only key change copies the index; the
   toggle is view state.
16. **Channels-window search** (D-175, proposed): the query is split like the names; loose matching is a substring per
   word, still in order.

17. **The shell's M2 wiring as built** (D-178, proposed): Options writes only the changed project options as one
   undoable command and adds the lap-stretch tolerance; the toolbar scheme selector and "follow OS" are user settings

5. **AC-M2-10 margin and the remaining cost** (D-191). The set is ready in 28-29 ms with the desktop busy (25-27 ms
   quieter), about 15 % under 33.3 ms. Its serial cost (`--workers 1`, Release, one core, per sample of each node's
   result): the store's seal of the output chunks (NaN scan, the eight pyramid levels, the summary copy, cache
   insertion, the chunk tasks) about 77 ms of the 171 ms the instrumented profile measured, 3.4-3.8 ns per sample,
   in `esses_store`; writing the chunks
   (the rounding fused into the conversion and the first touch of each new chunk block) about 30 ms, 1.4-1.8 ns;
   the evaluation itself 64 ms: gathers 0.3 ns per sample and input (no resampling occurs in RW-2: every node's
   inputs share its rate, so sharing gathered inputs across nodes would save under 3 ms), the elementwise
   interpreter 1.6 ns for `(a + b) / 2`, 3.1 ns for Rear Slip and 4.9 ns for `hypot` (division, comparisons,
   `choose`, `max` and `hypot` do not vectorise under MSVC), smooth 2.1 ns (one dependent add and subtract per
   sample: a prefix sum would change the rounding), the two-point derivative 1.1 ns, the RC filters 3.3 ns per sample
   for two interleaved passes and 6.4 ns for zero phase over the whole extent (one dependent multiply-add chain per
   pass), the order-4 Chebyshev 2.9 ns, integrate 0.7 ns, running_max with a gate and a reset 4.3 ns, flip_flop
   2.0 ns, time_shift 0.8 ns, the per-lap stat_max 9.7 ns, the range functions 0.1 ns. On 15 workers of 8 cores the
   set runs 6.2 times faster than serially; the critical path is the two-pass `filter_hp` node (13.1 ms alone). The
   next levers, in the order of their effect: a vectorised pyramid build and NaN scan and reuse of chunk blocks in
   `esses_store` (outside this work's scope), the v1.0 register bytecode for the elementwise operators (open issue
   24), then the chunk-parallel evaluator of v1.0 with checkpoints for the recursive filters (03 §6.1).
